# Fundamentals

A personal encyclopedia of computer science fundamentals, written in Markdown and built as I learn.

I study computer science, and I don't want anything I learn to be lost. Every concept I come across, in a project, a course or a talk, becomes a page here: defined simply, linked to related concepts, and backed by a reliable source. This repository grows with me.

> **Language.** The structure and documentation are in English. The notes themselves are written in French, and every concept also gives its English term.

## How to explore it

- **The [glossary](GLOSSARY.md)** lists every concept from A to Z, with a one-sentence definition.
- **Each domain** has its own home page and its own glossary.
- **Each page** links to related concepts and to its sources.
- The `status` field at the top of each page tells how far it has been checked: `draft`, `reviewed` or `verified` (see the [conventions](CONVENTIONS.md)).
